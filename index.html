About

Whatever’s on your mind right now – heartbreak, the boys, the girls, and everybody in between, the search for meaning or a longing for hope: Camona is here for you. She’s got R&B, soul and funk in her veins, the wind in her sails and an eight-piece turbo-band behind her. Her music is just like her: soft on the outside, tough on the inside, with a warm heart.


Numerous concerts and tours have taken her to established clubs such as Horns Erben in Leipzig and Blue Note in Dresden, as well as to festivals such as the Women in Jazz Festival Halle. With her tight rhythm section and the Brass-Extravaganza in tow, she rolls out the red carpet for the really big emotions
